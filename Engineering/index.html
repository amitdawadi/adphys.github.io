Engineering & Systems Integration Portfolio

Showcasing custom opto-mechanical instrumentation, high-speed data acquisition, and automated metrology systems.

1. Automated Opto-Mechanical Metrology System

Engineered a fully automated 3D laser-sheet profilometer capable of capturing surface height fields of 100-micron-thick materials. Eliminated manual sample transfers and completed hundreds of sequential test cycles with zero human intervention.

2. High-Speed DAQ & Sensor Integration

Designed a custom signal-amplifier circuit for a miniature force sensor, interfaced with a National Instruments ADC sampling at 40 kHz. Developed MATLAB routines to calibrate voltage against known weights and synchronize real-time force data with high-speed camera images.
